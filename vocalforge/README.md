# VocalForge — AI vocals for your lyrics

Type lyrics, pick a voice and a style, press Generate, and get them sung. Three outputs:

- **Full song** (default): sung vocals with backing music behind them.
- **Vocals only**: an a cappella take you can drop onto your own beat.
- **Instrumental**: backing music only, no vocals, built from the style settings.

Live at **https://jberry2347.github.io/vocalforge/**. It is a static page in this repo: no
build step, no backend. Audio is generated by the [ElevenLabs Music](https://elevenlabs.io/music)
API with **your own API key**, which stays in your browser.

## Getting started

1. Open the app and press the gear (or the "Add key" banner).
2. Paste an ElevenLabs API key. Make one at elevenlabs.io → your profile → **API keys**.
   Music generation needs a paid ElevenLabs plan; press **Check key** to see your remaining credits.
3. Write lyrics, set the voice and style, pick a length, and press **Generate vocals**.
   A take of 60 seconds usually arrives in well under a minute.
4. Every take lands in the right-hand list with a player, a download button and the exact
   request that produced it. Takes are stored in your browser only.

Credits: ElevenLabs charges per second of generated audio, so the length slider is also your
cost control. Drafting lyrics with "Let the model draft some" does not use credits.

## Writing lyrics

Sections start with a tag on its own line:

```
[Intro]

[Verse 1]
Streetlights hum on an empty road
Every mile a story told

[Chorus 18s]
Hold on, hold on, the night is ours
We're chasing down the falling stars

[Bridge 0:12]
...

[Outro]
```

- Recognised names: intro, verse, pre-chorus, chorus/hook, bridge, drop, solo/break, outro.
  Anything else is treated as a plain section.
- Add a length to pin a section: `[Chorus 18s]`, `[Bridge 0:12]`, `[Verse (20s)]`. Unpinned
  sections share whatever is left of the length slider, weighted by their line count.
- A tag with no lines under it becomes an instrumental passage (or wordless humming in
  vocals-only mode).
- Lines without a tag before them are treated as a verse.
- Limits from the API: at most 30 sections, 30 lines per section, 200 characters per line,
  3 seconds to 2 minutes per section, 10 minutes in total.

## Modes

**Structured** (default) sends one chunk per section through the API's composition plan, so
every section gets exactly the lyrics and length you wrote. With the legacy `music_v1` model
the plan is sent in the v1 "sections" format instead.

**Freeform** puts the lyrics inside a plain prompt and lets the model decide the structure.
Use it if you want the model to take more liberties, or if your lyrics have no sections.

## Rewriting a few lines without redoing the song

Every take made with **Keep this take editable** ticked is stored on ElevenLabs and gets an
"editable" badge. Press **Rewrite a section** on it, edit the lines you want changed, tick the
sections to regenerate, and press **Regenerate ticked sections**. The app sends a plan that keeps
every untouched section as a reference to the original audio and generates only the ticked ones,
so the rest of the song stays exactly as it was and only the regenerated seconds are charged. The
result lands as a new take named "… (rewrite)", itself editable again.

Notes:

- Section lengths stay the same on a rewrite. To change the structure, generate a new song.
- Rewriting needs the v2 or v2.5 model and a structured take. Freeform, instrumental and
  `music_v1` takes have no section map, so they can't be rewritten; use **Load settings** instead.
- A take made with the box unticked (or one ElevenLabs could not store) can still be rewritten:
  the app first uploads the original MP3 to ElevenLabs to get a song id. Both storing and uploading
  depend on your ElevenLabs plan; if either is refused, the error says so and the take stays as it is.

## Karaoke: word timing, LRC and SRT

Every take generated through the detailed endpoint (the default) asks ElevenLabs for the timing of
each sung word and keeps it with the take, shown as a "timed" badge. On such a take:

- **Karaoke** shows the lyrics under the player with the current word highlighted as it plays.
  Click any word to jump there.
- **Lyrics .lrc** downloads an enhanced LRC file, with a time tag on every word, named to match the
  MP3 so karaoke players and most music apps pick it up automatically.
- **Subtitles .srt** downloads one subtitle per lyric line for video editors.

Words are grouped into the lines you wrote. A take without timing (made with the plain endpoint, or
before this feature) shows **Get word timing**, which uploads the MP3 to ElevenLabs and asks for the
timestamps; whether that is allowed depends on your plan.

## If the browser blocks the call

The app calls `https://api.elevenlabs.io` straight from the page. If that is refused by the
browser (the app says "Could not reach https://api.elevenlabs.io" even though you are online),
deploy the small relay in [`relay/cloudflare-worker.js`](relay/cloudflare-worker.js) as a
Cloudflare Worker and put its URL into **Settings → API host**. The relay forwards requests
and adds the CORS headers; it does not store your key.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `index.html`, `app.css`, `app.js` | The app. All logic is in `app.js`; `window.VocalForge` exposes the lyric parser, request builder and multipart parser for tinkering. |
| `manifest.json`, `sw.js`, `icon*` | PWA bits so it installs as its own app and opens offline. |
| `privacy.html` | What is stored where. |
| `relay/cloudflare-worker.js` | Optional CORS relay, see above. |

## Running it locally

From the repo root:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080
# open http://localhost:8080/vocalforge/
```
